## Final Draft
To form an outline of how this overview of interop. will be approached it would be reasonable to build on the fundamental aspects of the field which act as the foundation to various legacy approaches in the implementation of polyglot systems. From this we can expand to how higher levels of abstractions are applied in terms of both the evolution of programming languages specifically focusing on their type systems and the corresponding execution models. The methodology here will be focused on aggregating and reviewing various instances of research and applications to demonstrate this overview. A focus will be placed on specifically how various aspects of interoperability build on top of one-another and tie into each-other, with a secondary focus on ensuring that sufficient topics are introduced to provide the background knowledge required to understand the motivating practical example, and give the reader the same insights into where it fits in the broader context of interoperability as I wished to gain with this research. Of note is that this is not meant to be a meta-analysis rather a survey which is compared and contrasted against a concrete practical implementation.
